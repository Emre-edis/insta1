# Privacy Policy for StoryCut

**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

**StoryCut** ("we", "our", or "the app") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when using the StoryCut Android application.

---

### 1. Zero Video Data Collection
- **100% Local Video Processing:** All video slicing, trimming, and watermarking operations are performed strictly on your local device hardware using the Android media framework.
- **No Uploads:** Your videos, photos, and media files are **NEVER** uploaded, transferred, or transmitted to any remote server, cloud service, or third party.

---

### 2. Permissions Used
- **Photos and Videos (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / Storage):** Required solely to let you choose videos from your device gallery and save the sliced 1-minute video segments back into your device's `Movies/StoryCut` album.
- **Internet & Network State:** Used exclusively to display Google AdMob advertisements to free users and to process Google Play In-App Billing transactions.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The app uses the following certified third-party service providers:
- **Google AdMob:** Free users are served banner and interstitial transition ads provided by Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID) and IP addresses in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Play In-App Billing:** Used for processing the one-time $2.99 Lifetime purchase to remove watermarks and all ads permanently. Google Play manages payment data securely; StoryCut does not collect, receive, or store credit card details or financial information.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
StoryCut does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
